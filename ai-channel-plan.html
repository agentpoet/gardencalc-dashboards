What

One channel, two cadences

Already confirmed it has legs — 18 real accounts run this exact format at $74K-477K/month. Scripted on-camera is the daily/weekly trunk; the AI-news episode is one segment inside it, not a second channel.

Why

Proof beats pitching

GardenCalc, HomeCalc, Phil's Roofing, Jack's Gym get named only in the moment they ARE the answer to a claim on screen. A viewer who checks the receipt finds it real — that is the whole sales pitch, no voiceover needed.
